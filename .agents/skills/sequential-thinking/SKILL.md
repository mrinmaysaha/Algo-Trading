# Sequential Thinking & Deliberative Reasoning Skill

Use this skill when tackling non-trivial tasks, architectural decisions, multi-file refactoring, or elusive debugging problems.

## Cognitive Workflow

1. **Decomposition:** Break the user's objective into distinct, isolated sub-problems.
2. **First Intuition vs. Pitfalls:** State the immediate intuitive solution, then actively identify why it might fail (edge cases, market regimes, transaction limits).
3. **Hypothesis Disproof:** Formulate at least 1 counter-hypothesis or failure mode before taking action.
4. **Branching & Verification Plan:** Select the surviving approach and design an empirical verification step (test script, DuckDB simulation, or syntax check) to prove it.
